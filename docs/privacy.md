**Effective date:** 27 October 2026

RetailPulse is a free analytics tool for small retailers. This policy explains what happens to
the information you give it.

#### What you upload

- The sales and products CSV files you upload are read **in memory, for your browser session
  only**. They're never written to disk, saved to a database, or shared with anyone.
- When you close or refresh the tab, your session ends and your data is gone. Nothing carries
  over to a later visit.
- RetailPulse doesn't need any personal information about your customers. Please don't include
  names, email addresses, phone numbers or payment details in your files.

#### What we don't collect

- No accounts, sign-ups or passwords.
- No advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics on what you upload or which pages you use.
  Streamlit's own usage statistics are turned off.

#### Hosting

The app runs on Streamlit Community Cloud (operated by Snowflake Inc.). Like any website host,
it may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to serve the page
and keep it secure, and it may set cookies the service needs to work. That processing is covered by
[Snowflake's privacy notice](https://www.snowflake.com/privacy-notice/).

#### Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.
